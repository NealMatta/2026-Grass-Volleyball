Rules

Vibes are everything. If your goal is to dominate every single game, this might not be the tournament for you.

The goal is to have a good time, make new friends, and play some competitive games.

Scoring

  • Pool games to 21, win by 2, cap 23
  • Bracket games to 25, win by 2, cap 27
  • Hard time limit. At the horn, whoever leads wins
  • Record both scores — differential decides seeding

Ball handling

  • No open-hand tipping — closed fist or 2+ knuckles
  • No set over unless your shoulders are square to the net
  • Setting on serve is allowed
  • Rotate positions to serve

Playing on grass

  • Sneakers, turf shoes or barefoot. No metal cleats
  • No antennas. Ball must cross between the poles. Pole or guy-line is out; net cable between the poles is live
  • Ball touching the line is in. No line and genuinely disputed? Replay it
  • Lets are live
  • Uneven ground: play it where it lies. Anyone may call an unsafe stop and replay the point

Refereeing

  • Not playing? You're refereeing. Two slots each
  • Referees handle score, rotation, in/out
  • Everything else is self-called — net touches, lifts, foot faults

Teams

  • Coed 4s. No gender requirement
  • Short-handed is fine — three is allowed
  • 5 minutes past start time is a forfeit

Tiebreakers

  • 1. Wins
  • 2. Head-to-head
  • 3. Point differential
  • 4. Points scored
  • 5. Rock paper scissors

Getting there & what to bring

AIDS Garden Chicago, 3003 N Lakefront Trail — Lake Shore Dr & Belmont, just south of Belmont Harbor.

Getting there

  • It's on the Lakefront Trail — no parking at the venue
  • Belmont Harbor lot is closest and fills early
  • CTA: Belmont Red/Brown/Purple, then #77 bus east
  • Biking is easily the least stressful option

Timing

  • 9:30 if you're on net setup
  • 10:00 first serve — slot 1 does not wait
  • 12:30 break, standings posted
  • Final at 1:15, done by 1:45

Definitely bring

  • Water. More than you think — no shade on the courts
  • Sunscreen
  • A ball if you have one

Bring if you want

  • Snacks to share
  • Drinks to share from Spike Bowl
  • A tent or canopy — the only shade we'll have
  • A football or a frisbee to toss around between games
  • A cooler, a speaker, camp chairs, a blanket

Nets

  • Court 1 — Deez Nets, set up with Bumping Buds
  • Court 2 — Tequila Mockingbird, set up with Perros Calientes
  • Only two nets exist. Michael and Grant, we're counting on you
